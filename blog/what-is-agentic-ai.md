# What Is Agentic AI? Understanding Autonomous AI Systems

**Target keyword:** what is agentic ai
**Word count:** ~1,500-2,000

---

Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can take autonomous actions — planning multi-step workflows, using tools, making decisions, and adapting to outcomes without constant human direction. Unlike conversational AI (chatbots), agentic AI acts on your behalf. It is the engineering paradigm powering AI agents like OpenClaw, Auto-GPT, and enterprise automation platforms.
