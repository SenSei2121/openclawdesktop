## Quick Answer

For personal AI automation: OpenClaw (free, self-hosted). For business process automation: n8n (visual, self-hostable). For developer workflows: LangChain (Python framework). For multi-agent teams: CrewAI. For simple integrations: Zapier (cloud, no-code).

## Use Case Recommendations

### "I want a personal AI assistant"
**Winner: OpenClaw**
Connects to your messaging apps, remembers your preferences, browses the web, manages files, and runs 24/7 on your hardware. No subscription, no data leaving your network.

### "I want to automate business processes without coding"
**Winner: n8n**
Visual drag-and-drop workflow builder with AI agent nodes and 400+ integrations. Self-hostable for data privacy. The market leader with 9.29M monthly visits.

### "I want to build custom AI applications"
**Winner: LangChain / LangGraph**
The deepest developer framework for AI applications. RAG, agents, multi-step workflows, evaluation, and production deployment. Best documentation.

### "I need multiple AI agents collaborating"
**Winner: CrewAI**
Role-based agents that plan, delegate, and collaborate. Researcher + Writer + Reviewer working together on complex tasks.

### "I want the simplest possible automation"
**Winner: Zapier**
6,000+ integrations, zero coding, cloud-hosted. If you just need "when X happens, do Y" with AI processing in the middle, Zapier is the most frictionless option.

### "I want maximum privacy"
**Winner: OpenClaw + Ollama**
Everything runs locally — AI model, assistant framework, and data storage. Zero internet dependency after initial setup. The most private option available.

## FAQ

**Can I switch between tools?**
Yes. AI models (Ollama, OpenAI, Anthropic) work with all listed tools. Your model files and API keys are reusable.

**Which is best for beginners?**
OpenClaw Manager (GUI) or Zapier (no-code). Both require zero coding or terminal experience.

**Which has the best community support?**
OpenClaw (348K GitHub stars) and n8n (100K stars) have the largest communities.

**Can I use multiple tools together?**
Yes. Common combinations: n8n for business workflows + OpenClaw as the AI assistant that n8n calls. Or LangChain for custom agents + OpenClaw for daily personal assistance.
