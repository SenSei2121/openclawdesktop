# Deploy AI Assistant on VPS: Remote Access for $4-15/Month

**Target keyword:** deploy ai assistant vps
**Word count:** ~1,500-2,000

---

A VPS (Virtual Private Server) gives you an always-on AI assistant accessible from anywhere — without running hardware at home. For $4-15/month, providers like Hetzner, DigitalOcean, or Contabo give you enough resources to run OpenClaw with a local model. Your data stays on infrastructure you rent, not on a cloud AI provider's servers.
